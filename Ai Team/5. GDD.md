PROJECT: TideLoop - Fishing Roguelite
VERSION: 1.0 - Written from phone

GENRE: 2D Cozy-Dark Fishing Roguelite + Time Loop

ELEVATOR PITCH:
You are a fisherman cursed to repeat 3 days. Ocean changes each loop. Catch 7 impossible fish to break curse. NPCs slowly remember you.

CORE LOOP:
1. Wake (Day 1/2/3) -> 2. Fish (timing mini-game) -> 3. Sell/Craft Bait -> 4. Explore islands/dialogue -> 5. Sleep -> Loop resets, world resets, but YOUR knowledge + fish collection persists

- 5 Fish Types with lore
