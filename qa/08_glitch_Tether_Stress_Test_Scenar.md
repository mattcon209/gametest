

**Tether Stress Test Scenarios**

1. **All Players Running Away in Opposite Directions**: Simulate maximum tension by having all players move away from the VIP in opposite directions.

2. **Three-Player Circle Entanglement**: Have three players circle around each other, creating a complex tangle of cords.

3. **Opposite Sides of Pillar Tangle**: Place two players on opposite sides of a pillar to cause cord crossing and entanglement.

4. **Untangling in Narrow Spaces**: Test scenarios where players must untangle while navigating through tight, confined areas.

5. **VIP Through Crowded Area**: The VIP moves through an area with numerous obstacles and other players, testing dynamic interactions.

6. **Multiple Players Pulling Simultaneously**: Have several players pull the VIP in the same direction to risk launch or significant movement.

7. **Jumping/Climbing While Tethered**: Encourage players to jump or climb while tethered to test cord dynamics under such movements.

8. **Extreme Tension Sprint**: All players sprint away from the VIP to create maximum tension and potential launches.

9. **Two-Player Entanglement Around Each Other**: Focus on two players trying to untangle while around each other, causing knotting.

10. **VIP in Water**: The VIP moves into water, altering buoyancy and movement, affecting tether behavior.

11. **All Players Pulling Opposite Directions**: Simulate extreme tension by having all players pull the VIP in opposite directions simultaneously.

12. **Players Anchoring on Objects**: Test scenarios where players anchor themselves using walls or objects while tethered.

13. **Three-Player Knot Formation**: Create a scenario where three players form a knot with their tethers, testing disentanglement mechanics.

14. **Sprinting Through Doors**: Players sprint through narrow doors while tethered, causing potential bunching and stress on cords.

15. **VIP Zigzag Path**: The VIP navigates a series of tight turns and zigzags, creating varied tether dynamics.

16. **All Players Pulling Simultaneously**: Have all players pull the VIP at once to test system limits for simultaneous interactions.

17. **Stationary Players vs Moving**: Some players stand still while others move, causing tension build-up from others' movement.

18. **Tethers Wrapping Around Moving Obstacles**: Introduce moving obstacles like cars or NPCs; have tethers wrap around them.

19. **Conflicting Redirections**: Multiple players attempt to redirect the VIP in opposite directions, testing coordination and outcomes.

20. **VIP Through Narrow Corridors**: The VIP moves through a series of narrow corridors with minimal space, forcing dynamic tether adjustments.

These scenarios are designed to thoroughly test the limits of the tether system under various stressors, ensuring comprehensive coverage of edge cases in multi-player interactions and environmental dynamics.
